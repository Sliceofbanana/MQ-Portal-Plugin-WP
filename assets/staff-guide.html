Walkthrough

Logging in

  1. Open the portal link the owner gave you on the shop computer or your phone. Log in with your email and password.
  2. No account yet? Tap Request an account. The owner approves it and gives you your position (Sales, Creatives, Production, QC Officer, Cashier, or Admin / Finance / Ops).
  3. On a phone, use the browser's Add to Home screen or Install app so the portal opens like an app.
  4. Check your name and mobile number in your name (top right) → My profile. Change your password there too.

Open this guide any time from Staff guide at the bottom of the menu, or from your name (top right).

You only see the tabs your position needs. If a tab you need is missing, ask the owner to check your position in Admin → Staff.

How an order moves

Every printing job, walk-in or online, becomes a job order. It moves through these stages, and each team only acts on its own stage.

Sales→ Creatives→ Production→ Quality Check→ Ready for Delivery→ Delivered
  • Sales sets the price, records the payment and fills in the specifications. Files that need checking or editing go to Creatives. Basic document printing that needs no checking goes straight to Production with Approve for Printing.
  • Creatives (only when needed) check the files, edit if needed, and approve them for printing.
  • Production prints and marks the job as printed.
  • QC checks the output. Approve, or reject it back to Production.
  • Release: notify the customer, then mark it delivered when they get it.

A job marked RUSH shows a red badge on every list and goes to the top of the queue.

Shop rules

These keep the shop's records complete. The Daily check page shows the owner when one is skipped.

Always
  • Log every sale. Walk-ins go in the POS; anything printed to order gets an invoice and a job order.
  • Print only from a job order or a POS receipt.
  • Write down the printer counter when the shop opens and closes.
  • Record the payment the moment you receive it, with the GCash or bank reference number.
  • Give the customer a receipt or their order number.
Never
  • Print for a customer "off the system", even small jobs.
  • Void a receipt or delete an order without a true reason. Every void and delete is logged with your name and emailed to the owner.
  • Share your login. Each action is saved under the person who is logged in.
SOP · Everyone

Time clock

  1. When you arrive, connect to the shop Wi-Fi and turn on your phone's location.
  2. Open Time Clock and tap Clock in. Allow the location request.
  3. For a break, tap Start break, then End break when you return.
  4. Before you leave, tap Clock out.

"You need to be at the shop to clock in" means the portal could not confirm you are at the shop. Connect to the shop Wi-Fi and allow location, then try again. If it still fails, tell the owner the time you arrived.

SOP · Sales

Handling an online order

Customers order on the website in 3 steps and do not pay yet. They get an order number that starts with MQ- and use it when they message the Facebook page.

  1. A new order shows on Home under "New online orders to price", and you get an email. Open Sales → Online orders and click the order. Orders with status New have no price yet.
  2. Read the Files table. Each file shows what it is for, the page count for documents, and the customer's complete instructions (copies, colored or B&W, size, paper). Very large files may come as a screenshot plus a Google Drive link.
  3. Click Set the price (invoice). Fill in the price for each line and save.
  4. Tap Email to customer on the invoice, or message them on the Facebook page with the order number and the price. The customer also sees the price and the payment QR codes on their tracking page.
  5. When they send proof of payment, record it on the invoice. See Recording payments.
  6. Click Open job order (files, RUSH). Check the specifications and tap Mark as RUSH if the customer needs it urgently.
  7. Choose where it goes next:
    • Files need checking or editing: tick The file requires review/checking/editing and tap Send to Creatives →.
    • Basic document printing only: tick Basic document printing only and No need for Creatives to review/check/edit, then tap Approve for Printing. It goes straight to Production.

Delivery: "Pick up" means they come to MQ Printing Services Main Office; the order page tells them to message the Facebook page for a pick-up time. "Courier" means the customer books Maxim, Lalamove or Grab once you tell them the order is ready. You do not book the rider. The order page tells them to have the rider call 09205177884 on arrival.

SOP · Sales

Walk-in customer or quotation

  1. Go to Sales → Invoice Manager. Tap Create quotation if the customer is still deciding, or Create invoice if they are ordering now.
  2. Pick the brand and branch, then search for the customer or add a new one (name, contact number, email).
  3. Add each item with its description and specifications, quantity and unit price. Set the due date.
  4. Save. Use Print / PDF or Email to customer to send it.
  5. When the customer agrees, open the quotation and tap → Invoice. The invoice creates the job order.
  6. Record the payment and complete the specifications on the job order. Then send it to Creatives, or tap Approve for Printing for basic document printing (see Online orders, last step).

Small over-the-counter items (photocopy, a few prints, office supplies paid on the spot) go through the POS instead.

SOP · Sales

Customer uploads

  1. Open Sales → Customer uploads. New files show under New.
  2. Download and check the file.
  3. Turn it into a quotation or invoice. The upload moves to Converted to document.
  4. If it is spam or a duplicate, tap Dismiss.
SOP · Sales

Recording a payment

  1. Open the invoice in Sales → Invoice Manager (or from the online order).
  2. Tap Record payment. Enter the amount, the method (cash, GCash, bank) and the reference number from the customer's screenshot.
  3. Tap Save payment. The job order's payment check turns green.

Recording a payment on a quotation or billing statement creates the invoice and its job order automatically. If the internet is down, the payment saves on the device and syncs later.

SOP · Creatives

Checking files

  1. Open Creatives. Jobs with a red RUSH badge go first.
  2. Open the job order and download the customer's files. Read the specifications and the customer's notes.
  3. File is fine: tap Approve for printing →.
  4. File needs work: tap Needs editing, write what needs fixing, make the edit, attach the edited design to the job order, then approve.
  5. Something missing or unclear (wrong size, missing file): tap ← Back to Sales and say what is needed.
SOP · Production

Printing a job

  1. Open Production & QC. The Production queue lists approved jobs, RUSH first.
  2. Open the job order and download the approved design. Print exactly what the specifications say.
  3. Tap Printed / produced → send to QC.

If QC rejects the job, it comes back to your queue with QC's note. Fix it and mark it printed again.

SOP · QC Officer

Quality check

  1. Open Production & QC. Jobs waiting are under Quality check.
  2. Compare the output with the specifications: size, quantity, colors, paper, cut and finish.
  3. All good: tap APPROVED / READY FOR DELIVERY.
  4. Problem found: tap Reject → back to Production and write what is wrong.
SOP · Sales / QC

Release and pick-up

  1. Open the job under Ready for delivery.
  2. Tap Copy message. The "order ready" message already has the customer's name and order number; edit it first if you need to. Paste it in SMS, Messenger or Viber and send it. The portal does not send it for you.
  3. Choose how you told them (SMS, Call, Messenger, Email or Viber) and tap Customer notified. For courier orders, tell them to book their rider now. The rider calls 09205177884 on arrival.
  4. When they pick it up, check the balance. Collect it and record it on the invoice first.
  5. Tap Mark as delivered ✓. If a balance is still unpaid the portal warns you; only continue if the owner allowed it.

Jobs released with a balance are listed on the owner's Daily check.

SOP · Cashier

A POS sale

  1. Open POS → Register. Check the branch at the top.
  2. Tap items to add them, adjust the quantity, or add a Custom item.
  3. Tap Charge. Choose the payment method; for cash, enter the cash received and give the change shown.
  4. Keep Print receipt ticked and tap Complete sale. Hand the receipt to the customer.

Offline? The sale saves on the device and syncs when the internet is back. Reprint a receipt from POS → Today's receipts → Reprint.

SOP · Cashier

Opening and closing the shop

When the shop opens

  1. Open POS → Printer counter.
  2. For each printer, read the total page counter from the printer's screen or report and save it.

When the shop closes

  1. Write down the printer counters again in POS → Printer counter.
  2. Open POS → End of day. Count the cash and compare it with the cash total. Check GCash and bank totals against the payments received.
  3. Report any difference to the owner right away.

A counter lower than the last one (after a printer reset or repair) asks for a note. Explain what happened.

SOP · Admin / Owner

The daily check

Every morning the owner gets an email with yesterday's numbers. The same report is on Admin → Daily check, for any date.

  1. Look at Money in: POS sales and invoice payments for the day.
  2. Look at Printer counter vs pages sold. A flagged gap means more pages were printed than sold. Ask the shop what was printed.
  3. Check Voided receipts, voided documents and deleted online orders. Each shows who did it and why.
  4. Check Online orders without a price and Customer uploads waiting: customers waiting for a reply.
  5. Check Job orders released with a balance.
  6. If a printer has no counter for the day, remind the cashier.
SOP · Admin / Owner

Deleting an order or voiding a receipt

  • Delete an online order (Admin and Owner only): open it in Sales → Online orders, tap Delete order and type the reason. This removes the order, its invoice, job order and files. An order with a recorded payment cannot be deleted.
  • Void a POS receipt: POS → Today's receipts → Void receipt, with a reason. Only staff allowed to void see this.
  • Void a quotation or invoice: open it in the Invoice Manager and void it, with a reason.

Every delete and void is saved in the activity log with the name and reason, shows on the Daily check, and is emailed to the admins.

Help

When you are confused

"Send to Creatives" or "Approve for Printing" is greyed out

The job order shows two checklists and what is missing in each. Both need payment received, specifications complete and a sales person assigned. Send to Creatives also needs The file requires review/checking/editing ticked. Approve for Printing needs Basic document printing only and No need for Creatives to review/check/edit ticked. Record the payment or finish the specifications, then try again.

I cannot find a job order

Use the search box in Sales → Job orders with the customer name, job order number, or the online order number (MQ-…). Set the stage filter to "All". Sales staff only see their own job orders unless allowed to see all.

A customer messaged with an MQ- number

That is their online order number. Search it in Sales → Online orders.

The customer uploaded the wrong file type

The order page accepts PDF for documents and print-and-bind, and PDF, JPEG, PNG or PSD for everything else, up to 100 MB per file. Files too large can be sent as a screenshot plus a Google Drive link. If they have another format, ask them to email it to info@mqprintsph.com with their name and order number, then attach it to the job order.

The portal says I'm offline

POS sales and payments still save on the device and sync when the internet returns. Clocking in and out needs the internet.

The receipt did not print

Check the printer has paper and is on. Go to POS → Today's receipts and tap Reprint. In the print window, pick the thermal printer.

I pressed the wrong button on a job

Do not try to fix it by pressing more buttons. Tell the owner or admin with the job order number. Every step is in the job's history, so it can be put back.

A tab or button I need is missing

Your position decides what you see. Ask the owner to check your position in Admin → Staff.

I forgot my password

Ask the owner to set a new password for you in Admin → Staff. Change it to your own in My profile → Password after you log in.
